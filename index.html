Before work begins

Line up every written quote before you choose.

Written quotes for one job can read very differently. For a home with a driveway or parking pad but no garage, they may differ on outside mounting, weather exposure and the cable route from an indoor panel to an exterior wall.

Give each contractor the same description, then compare scope, inclusions, notification, restoration and exclusions line by line. A lower or higher total means little until you know which items each quote actually covers.

How do I compare two written electrical quotes fairly?

Ask each contractor to price the same described scope, then set the quotes side by side. Check inclusions, exclusions, restoration of opened surfaces, the ESA notification and what timing and payment terms each one states.

What should I do if one quote leaves out the ESA notification?

Ask the contractor to state in writing who files it and whether it is included in the stated amount. A quote that is silent on notification is not directly comparable with one that covers it.

Does a driveway with no garage change what the quotes should cover?

It can. Without a garage, a charger or outdoor receptacle may be mounted on an exterior wall or a post, so ask each quote to describe the mounting, weather protection and cable route.

Which records help when contractors give different answers?

Photograph the panel door and its labels from the outside, and collect any inspection report, insurer letter or earlier ESA paperwork. Sharing the same documents with every contractor makes differences in their answers easier to explain.
